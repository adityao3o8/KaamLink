# KaamLink FAQ (sample)

> Sample content for demo; replace with official notifications.

## How do I find work on KaamLink?
Open "I need work", record or type your name, trade (mason/painter/plumber/electrician/helper/carpenter), area, and expected daily wage, then Confirm & save. Contractors can then match and alert you.

## How do I hire workers?
Open "I need workers", speak or type a job like "Kal 3 mason chahiye Chengalpattu 800", review the extracted job, match workers, then send wave alerts. Workers reply Yes/No.

## What trades are supported?
Mason (mestri / raj mistri), painter (colour wala), plumber (pipe wala), electrician (wireman), helper (mazdoor), carpenter (wood wala).

## What languages work?
Hindi, Tamil, English, and mixed Hinglish / Tanglish voice notes and text.

## Why is my wage flagged as low?
KaamLink compares your offer to typical wages for that skill and area from past jobs. If it is below about 85% of the median, it shows a lowball warning so you can adjust.

## How do alerts work?
Top workers are alerted in waves of 5. If someone declines or times out, the next workers are alerted until the required count is filled. Phone numbers unlock only after Yes.

## Can I browse workers without posting a job?
Yes. Use Browse available workers to filter by skill (for example all masons), area, and availability date.

## What if wages are unpaid?
Politely remind the contractor in writing with dates and amount, keep proof, and seek local labour help if needed. KaamLink does not process payments.
