# Construction site safety (sample)

> Sample content for demo; replace with official notifications.

Wear a helmet, sturdy shoes, and gloves when the site requires them.
Do not work on unsafe scaffolding or heights without proper support.
Report damaged tools, open pits, and exposed electrical wires to the contractor immediately.
Stay hydrated and take short breaks in extreme heat.
Never operate machines you have not been trained for.
Keep emergency contact numbers saved on your phone.
