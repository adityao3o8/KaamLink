# How matching works (sample)

> Sample content for demo; replace with official notifications.

Workers are filtered by skill and availability date first.
Then KaamLink ranks by embedding similarity, reliability, wage fit, and area match.
Alerts go out in waves of five. If someone declines or times out, the next-ranked worker is alerted.
Phone numbers are shown to the contractor only after a worker accepts.
Reliability improves when contractors leave good ratings after completed jobs.
