# Fair hiring tips for contractors (sample)

> Sample content for demo; replace with official notifications.

State the wage, location, and dates clearly in the first voice note.
Avoid offering far below the typical range for that trade and area — KaamLink will flag lowball offers.
Confirm workers promptly so they can plan travel.
Pay on the agreed day and keep communication polite.
Rate workers fairly after the job so good workers rise in future shortlists.
