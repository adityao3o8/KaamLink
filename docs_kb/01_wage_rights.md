# Wage rights tips (sample)

> Sample content for demo; replace with official notifications.

Workers should agree the daily wage clearly before starting work.
Ask for the rate per day, overtime rules, and who pays travel if the site is far.
Keep a simple written note or WhatsApp message of the agreed wage and dates.
If the offered wage seems unusually low for your trade and area, ask for a fair rate before accepting.
KaamLink can show typical wage ranges from past jobs — use that as a discussion starting point, not a legal minimum.
