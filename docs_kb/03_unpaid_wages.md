# What to do about unpaid wages (sample)

> Sample content for demo; replace with official notifications.

First, politely remind the contractor in writing (WhatsApp is fine) with dates and amount due.
Save attendance notes, photos of the site, and any wage agreement messages.
Ask a trusted colleague or mestri to witness the conversation if possible.
If payment is still delayed, contact a local labour help desk, union, or legal aid centre for guidance.
KaamLink does not process payments — it only helps with matching and alerts.
This note is general information, not legal advice.
